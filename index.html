Cocodona 250 Pacer Rules & Community Recommendations

Aravaipa 2027 Guidelines
Official Pacer Windows

Pacing begins strictly at Mile 75.6 (Whiskey Row). The first 75.6 miles are solo for all runners. There are approximately 178 miles remaining to the finish line where pacing is permitted.

Strict No-Pacer Zones

1. Schnebly Hill (mi 175.7) to Munds Park (mi 190.1): No pacers on course.
2. Wildcat Hill (mi 234.2) to Heritage Square Finish (mi 253.5): Aravaipa prohibits pacers on the final ~19.3 miles.

Recommended Pacer Count

Ultra community consensus recommends 3 to 5 pacers as the golden middle ground (~25-45 mi/pacer). 2 pacers is ultra-heavy (50-70 mi each), while 6 pacers offers fresh, bite-sized shifts (~15-30 mi each).

Rules Note: Pacers must check in at their starting aid station, wear an official pacer bib, be 18+, carry mandatory gear (GPS/phone with track loaded, 4L where required), and cannot mule gear for runners.
1 Pacer per Runner on trail

The Reliable 3-Hour Fueling Loop

Repeat this cycle every 3 hours while moving to eliminate stomach shutdown:

0:25 into run: Gel / Chews (~25g carbs) + 4-6 sips water
0:55 into run: Liquid Electrolyte sip (~200mg sodium) + small real food bite
1:25 into run: Second Gel or stroopwafel (~25g carbs)
1:55 into run: Real solid carbs (boiled potato with salt, tortilla, pretzel)
2:25 into run: Gel / Chews (~25g carbs)
2:55 into run: Broth / Soup / Salt cap at upcoming aid station

Aid Station Real Food Strategy

After 24+ hours, sweet gel fatigue is inevitable. Keep these real food staples ready in crew bins:

Salty & Savory
  • Boiled baby potatoes rolled in sea salt
  • Chicken / ramen broth (hydrates + sodium)
  • Quesadillas & bacon strips
  • Avocado salted sourdough slices
Easy Digestion Carbs
  • White rice balls with soy sauce
  • Applesauce / baby food pouches
  • Watermelon cubes with salt
  • Canned peaches / mandarin oranges
Post-Sleep Anchor Meals
  • Scrambled eggs + hash browns
  • Pancakes with maple syrup
  • Chocolate milk + banana
  • Greek yogurt with granola & honey
Stomach Reset Protocols
  • Ginger chews / ginger ale
  • Peppermint tea
  • Pickle juice sips (cramp relief)
  • Slow down pace 10% until GI settles
